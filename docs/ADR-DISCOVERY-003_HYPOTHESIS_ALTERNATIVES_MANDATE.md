# ADR-DISCOVERY-003: Compulsory Hypotheses and Alternative Explanations

**Version 1.0.0** - Architecture Decision Record  
**Status**: Canonical Standard - Forgery Protocol  
**Domain**: Scientific Hypothesis Formulation

---

## 1. Context

An isolated hypothesis lacks differential explanatory power if it is not measured against a range of alternative explanations (null hypotheses, competing mechanisms, measurement artifacts or orthogonal hypotheses). Robust science advances through the competitive elimination of rival alternatives (strong inference).

---

## 2. Architectural Decision

1. **Mandatory Alternative Requirement**:
   - No hypothesis will be admitted to the knowledge graph or processed by the prediction engines if it does not explicitly define its set of **Alternatives (`alternatives`)**.
   - The space of alternatives must include at least:
     * The null hypothesis or technical/statistical artifact.
     * At least one alternative causal mechanism already known or proposed in the literature.
     * An orthogonal or opposing mechanism generated by the system.

2. **Differentiability of Predictions**:
   - For each alternative formulated, the system must identify at least one differential prediction (`discriminative_prediction`): an experiment or observation where the main hypothesis predicts the outcome $A$ while the alternative predicts $B$.

---

## 3. Consequences

### Positive
- It guarantees that the hypotheses are neither trivial nor truisms.
- It allows experiments and empirical queries to be guided towards tests of maximum discrimination (optimal reduction of epistemic entropy).
- Direct alignment with the 'A' (Alternatives) pillar of the TRACES framework.

### Negatives / Trade-offs
- Requires prior computational effort to explore and populate the space of concurrent hypotheses before proceeding with detailed investigation.

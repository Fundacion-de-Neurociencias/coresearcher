# Type D Hypotheses Examples (Discovery-Level)

Estos son ejemplos de hypotheses Type D - las que debería generar CoResearcher para validar la visión de "Scientific Discovery OS".

## Alzheimer's Disease

> **"Amyloid-beta deposition is not the cause but a protective response to early-stage neurodegeneration, attempting to sequester toxic oligomers."**

*Reframe*: Invierte la causalidad - la patología como respuesta protectora, no como causa.

## ALS

> **"What if TDP-43 pathology is not the cause of ALS but a protective stress response where motor neurons attempt to sequester toxic RNA aggregates?"**

*Reframe*: La inclusión proteica como mecanismo adaptativo, no patológico.

## Parkinson's Disease

> **"What if alpha-synuclein aggregates are not the cause of Parkinson's but an adaptive response to dopaminergic stress?"**

*Reframe*: La agregación proteica como respuesta adaptativa.

## Rare Diseases

> **"What if the lack of phenotype in some genetic knockouts is not due to redundancy but to an adaptive rewiring that creates an emergent property only visible under stress conditions?"**

*Reframe*: Redundancia → Reprogramación adaptativa.

## Drug Repurposing

> **"What if the CNS effects of statins are not due to direct brain penetration but to a peripheral signaling cascade that crosses the blood-brain barrier?"**

*Reframe*: Acción directa → señalización periférica.

## Cardiovascular Disease

> **"What if PCSK9's cardiovascular benefit is not mediated through LDL cholesterol but through a novel immunomodulatory pathway?"**

*Reframe*: Metabolismo lipídico → inmunomodulación.

## Autoimmune Diseases

> **"What if the variable response to environmental triggers is mediated by a previously unknown connection between microbiome-derived metabolites and thymic selection?"**

*Reframe*: Genética pura → microbioma + selección tímica.

## Longevity

> **"What if intermittent fasting benefits are not due to metabolic switching but to a circadian reprogramming of stem cell niches?"**

*Reframe*: Metabolismo → reprogramación circadiana.

## Huntington's Disease

> **"What if the cognitive delay in Huntington's is not due to slower progression but to a compensatory network that maintains function until collapse?"**

*Reframe*: Progresión lenta → compensación activa.

---

## Patrón Común

Todas las hypotheses Type D comparten:

1. **"What if"** - Pregunta paradójica
2. **"not... but"** - Reframing causal
3. **Mecanismo oculto** - Conexión no obvia
4. **Consecuencia no intuitiva** - Sorpresa científica

## Métricas Clave

- **Novelty Score**: 0.9
- **Surprise Factor**: Alto (presence de "protective", "adaptive", "what if")
- **Validation Path Clarity**: Media-Alta
- **Breakthrough Potential**: Sí

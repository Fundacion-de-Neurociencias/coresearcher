# Sprint 40 — Manual Evidence Collection

## Objective
Determine whether intellectual history is consistently present across scientific repositories.

## Method
For each repository, select:
- 15 historical issues (oldest high-comment)
- 10 merged pull requests
- 5 controversial or long-running discussions

For each item document:
1. Original problem
2. Proposed solutions
3. Failed attempts
4. Final decision
5. Evidence of scientific reasoning
6. Evidence of disagreement
7. Evidence of learning

## Repositories
1. MNE-Python (mne-tools/mne-python)
2. Nilearn (nilearn/nilearn)
3. PyBIDS (bids-standard/pybids)

- artifacts/sprint39c_mne_replication.md
- artifacts/sprint39c_nilearn_replication.md
- artifacts/sprint39c_pybids_replication.md

Evidence collection only.
